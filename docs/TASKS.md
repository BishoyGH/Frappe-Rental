> T10 notes: rate rows overlap when time unit, priority, season, customer group and unit match, weekdays share a day, dates intersect and unit ranges `[min, max)` intersect (max 0 = open); an open-ended base row therefore needs a `max_units` or a different priority next to duration tiers. Different seasons count as disjoint by name (their date ranges are not compared). Adds Rental Season, and the deposit-rule and cancellation-policy links on Rental Type (earlier than the T06 note said) plus `rate_card` on Rental Item. Cancellation tiers are sorted longest notice first.

> T11 notes: `quote(request, card)` takes plain dicts (loading the card and context is T12's job). Rate choice: highest priority, then most specific (unit > customer group > season), then the cheapest total. Minute and Hour count real elapsed time; Day and Week count local wall-clock time, so a night across a DST change is one night; Month and Year use calendar months with the remainder pro-rated over the following month. Group and coupon discounts compound; percent charges apply after discounts; tax is one rate on the net total (per-item tax templates are applied when the Sales Invoice is built in T16). Coupons are passed in as a percent; resolving ERPNext Coupon Codes happens in T12. Hooks: `rental_pricing_hooks` in hooks.py, each `fn(request, result) -> result`.

> T12 notes: statuses exist on the doctype but transitions (Hold, Confirm, Cancel) are T14; availability is T13. Each line is priced from its Rental Item's rate card; booking-level charges come from each line's card, and manual charge rows (`is_manual`) survive repricing. Line `discount_pct` is the customer-group discount slot of the pricing engine; the coupon percent comes from the Coupon Code's Pricing Rule when it is a Discount Percentage rule. Tax is the sum of the template's On Net Total rows on the booking net total; full ERPNext tax calculation happens on the Sales Order/Invoice in T16. The Rental Type time unit Night is priced and validated as Day. Datetimes are treated as UTC and converted to the location timezone for pricing.

> T13 notes: `services/availability.py` provides `is_available`, `free_units`, `reserve_booking`, `reserve_block` and `release`; T14 calls them on hold, confirm and cancel. Writers lock the Rental Unit row and then use a locking read of the cache, because a plain read under REPEATABLE READ missed a parallel writer's rows (the concurrency test caught this). Units are locked before any plain read, because MariaDB 11.8 snapshot isolation rejects a locking read of rows changed after the snapshot (error 1020); local MariaDB 10.11 needs `innodb_snapshot_isolation=ON` to reproduce it. Pooled units are checked by peak concurrent quantity, not the sum of all overlapping rows. A Rental Block fills the unit completely, whatever its mode. The "capacity" case is covered by pooled `quantity_available`; a separate per-person capacity check (Rental Item `capacity_people`) is not enforced yet.
